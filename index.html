Table Grubs: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Table Grubs is a mixed reality game for Meta Quest made by Frederick Coles ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game does and doesn't do with your information.

The short version

Table Grubs does not collect, store or share any personal information about you. There are no adverts, no analytics and no accounts of our own.

What stays on your headset

  • Your progress and settings (your Grubs, runs, records and options) are saved only on your Quest.
  • Where the game sits on your table is remembered with a Quest spatial anchor, which is stored on your headset by the Quest system.

We cannot see any of this, and it is removed if you uninstall the game.

Cameras, microphone and your room

The game shows your real room through the headset's passthrough, which is handled by the Quest system. The game never reads, records or stores camera images. It does not use the microphone and does not scan your room.

Meta

When the game starts, it asks Meta's platform to confirm that your account owns the game. This check is handled by Meta, and your use of Meta Quest and the Meta Horizon Store is covered by Meta's own privacy policy.

Children

We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because the game collects no personal information at all.

Changes

If the game ever starts collecting information (for example, an online feature), we will update this policy before that update is released.
